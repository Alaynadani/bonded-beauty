## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-fashion editorial intimacy and warm modern minimalism, tailored for an upscale boutique salon experience. Rejecting cold sterile clinical aesthetics and murky dark backdrops, the interface relies on luminous alabaster and ivory foundations contrasted with razor-sharp espresso architecture, soft champagne luster, and nuanced terracotta rose accents.

The visual style merges editorial high-contrast discipline with organic hospitality. Visual hierarchy is achieved through generous whitespace, razor-thin hairlines, and classical high-contrast typography, evoking the tactile luxury of heavyweight linen paper, curated lookbooks, and serene salon interiors.

## Colors

The palette creates a sunlit, textural luxury experience anchored in deep, warm espresso tones.

- **Primary (`#241E1C`)**: Deep roasted espresso black. Acts as the primary anchor for typography, structural borders, and high-impact call-to-action buttons.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Muted champagne gold. Used for subtle metadata, decorative rule accents, selected state highlights, and understated luxury badges.
- **Tertiary (`#B87A68`)**: Terracotta rose. Applied with deliberate restraint to interactive highlights, notifications, promotional flags, and service category accents.
- **Neutral Background (`#FAF8F5`)**: Radiant alabaster cream. Provides the foundational canvas, replacing harsh digital white with warm, paper-like warmth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F5F1EA`)**: Warm linen tone used for card backgrounds, inputs, dropdown menus, and nested service tiers.
- **Hairline Borders (`#E5DFD5`)**: Delicate micro-borders that structure cards, grids, and dividers without cluttering the editorial canvas.

## Typography

The typographical pairing evokes the rhythm of independent art and fashion magazines:

- **Headlines (Bodoni Moda)**: Neoclassical high-contrast serif with pronounced vertical stress and razor serifs. Sets an authoritative, poetic tone for studio storytelling, section headers, and hero titles. Always preserve breathing room above and below.
- **Body & Captions (Manrope)**: Geometric yet warm grotesque sans-serif with open apertures and high legibility at dense scales. Handles service descriptions, pricing matrices, scheduling information, and fine print.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels**: Uppercase tracking (0.12em to 0.16em) in `Manrope` transforms navigational tags, service categories, and subheadings into crisp architectural markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure adheres to an intentional, spacious editorial grid designed to feel unhurried and calm:

- **Grid System**: 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max-width 1360px centered), condensing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile devices.
- **Section Rhythm**: Generous vertical cadences (`space-3xl` on desktop, `space-2xl` on mobile) allow photography and typography to command the canvas without feeling stacked or compressed.
- **Gutter & Margins**: Margins scale gracefully from generous perimeter borders on desktop (`margin: 4rem`) down to compact safe areas (`margin-mobile: 1.25rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, mimicking tangible high-end print collateral:

- **Flat Tonal Layering**: Depth is achieved via shifts between `#FAF8F5` (canvas root) and `#F5F1EA` (cards, floating sheets, inputs).
- **Precision Micro-Hairlines**: Layers are defined by crisp 1px borders in `#E5DFD5`, ensuring separation without visual heaviness.
- **Subtle Ambient Lift (Modals & Overlays)**: For dropdown menus, mobile drawers, and booking sheets, a whisper-soft diffused shadow is applied: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.07)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by tailored soft edges:

- **Primary Geometry (`roundedness: 1`)**: Standard elements like cards, containers, text fields, and primary buttons use a refined 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius, preserving crisp editorial structure.
- **Specialized Circular Elements**: Category filter pills, avatar thumbnails, and small status badges utilize full rounded geometry (`9999px`) to create clear tactile contrast against rectangular content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons & Booking CTAs
- **Primary CTA ("Book Appointment")**: Solid `#241E1C` espresso fill with crisp white text (`Manrope`, 12px, bold, uppercase, tracked 0.12em), 4px radius, padded `14px 28px`. Hover: shifts to `#3A3230` with subtle translateY(-1px).
- **Secondary / Outline Button**: Hairline border in `#241E1C` (1px) over transparent or `#FAF8F5` surface with `#241E1C` label. Hover: inverts to solid `#241E1C` with white text.
- **Text Button / Editorial Link**: `#241E1C` with a persistent or animated 1px underline positioned 4px below text baseline.

### Service Category Pills
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `6px 14px`.
- **Default State**: Translucent `#E5DFD5` border, warm linen `#F5F1EA` background, `#241E1C` text.
- **Active State**: Solid `#241E1C` fill with white text, or accent terracotta border with tint fill.

### Service & Pricing Cards
- Enclosed in subtle 1px `#E5DFD5` borders with `#F5F1EA` interior.
- Title in `Bodoni Moda` (22px), service duration and pricing set in uppercase `Manrope` muted labels. Includes nested hairline dividers separating individual add-ons or tiers.

### Testimonial Cards
- Generous internal padding (`2rem`).
- Large decorative quotes or quotation typography in `Bodoni Moda` italic, followed by body text in `Manrope` (15px/24px) in muted espresso (`#4A4340`), ending with client name and service received in tracked uppercase champagne/terracotta.

### Sleek Booking Banner
- Top or sticky footer format rendered in rich espresso `#241E1C` with `#FAF8F5` text.
- Pairs an informational micro-label ("Midland Atelier & Studio") with an immediate high-contrast booking link and subtle champagne gold accents.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Clean `#FAF8F5` or transparent background with a 1px border in `#E5DFD5`.
- Active focus state transitions border to `#241E1C` without harsh outer glow rings.
