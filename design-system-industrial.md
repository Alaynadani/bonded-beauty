## Brand & Style

This design system channels an upscale urban atelier: raw blackened steel, architectural concrete tones, and glowing burnished amber accents. The aesthetic marries raw industrial minimalism with tailored, high-end salon craftsmanship. It feels uncompromisingly modern, disciplined, and bespoke.

The target audience seeks deliberate artistry, precision grooming, and avant-garde style in an environment that rejects clinical sterility in favor of heavy materials, tactile metals, and architectural weight.

Visual execution rests on high structural contrast, disciplined geometric sans typography, dark monolithic containers, and precise hairline boundaries that evoke custom metalwork and raw architectural frames.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by blackened industrial neutrals and energized by warm glowing metallics.

- **Primary (`#D97706`)**: Burnished amber. Used intentionally for active states, key visual calls-to-action, structural highlights, and focal interactive accents.
- **Secondary (`#E5A93C`)**: Brushed bronze. Used for secondary emphasis, active indicators, badges, and glowing highlights against dark backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#DCDCD8`)**: Concrete neutral. Serves as high-contrast structural text, crisp borders, and subtle muted surfaces when inverted.
- **Neutral (`#121214`)**: Raw carbon steel. The foundational root surface, paired with `#1E1E22` for stacked elevation layers and `#2A2A30` for structural dividing frames.

Text readability strictly preserves WCAG AAA contrast for primary content using crisp concrete white (`#F2F2F0`) against dark surfaces, reserving amber and bronze tints for purposeful visual tension.

## Typography

Typography establishes an architectural, editorial voice. 

- **Headlines (`Montserrat`)**: Geometric, sharp, and commanding. Rendered with tight negative letter tracking and heavy weights to mirror metal plate etchings and structural brutalism. Display text conveys deliberate luxury and high-fashion edge.
- **Body & Editorial Copy (`Work Sans`)**: Grounded, functional, and balanced. Provides legible rhythm for service menus, stylist biographies, consultation notes, and technical product breakdowns.
- **Labels & Microcopy (`Montserrat`)**: Uppercase letterforms paired with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) deliver precise utilitarian contrast for metadata, pricing, status markers, and interactive controls.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a high-density 4-column system on mobile devices. The rhythm combines expansive negative margins with tightly clustered interior element groupings to reflect an industrial atelier layout.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 3rem exterior boundary margins. Asymmetrical content splits allow treatment menus and imagery to sit alongside architectural data panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2rem margins.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem margins. Stacked monolithic blocks maximize touch targets without losing structural definition.

Component interior spacing adheres strictly to the 4px base increment (`0.25rem`), balancing tight information density with airy section breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through tonal surface stepping and sharp metal-etched borders rather than diffuse drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (`#121214`)**: Raw charcoal carbon slate backing canvas.
- **Level 1 Containers (`#1E1E22`)**: Structural cards, salon station modules, and service blocks framed by a 1px border of `#2A2A30`.
- **Level 2 Flyouts & Modals (`#26262B`)**: Booking drawers, time pickers, and filter menus bordered by `#383840` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`).
- **Active / Accent Glow**: Interactive focus points use a restrained burnished amber line glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #D97706, 0 0 16px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`).

Diffuse, muddy shadows are avoided; edges remain disciplined and architectural.

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained `roundedness: 1` scale (base radius `0.25rem` / 4px).

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Cards)**: 4px corner radius (`rounded-sm`). This micro-softening removes the razor sharpness of 0px brutalism while retaining an industrial, milled-steel aesthetic.
- **Containers & Modals (`rounded-lg`)**: 8px (0.5rem) maximum radius.
- **Interactive Tabs & Toggles (`rounded-xl`)**: 12px (0.75rem) reserved exclusively for nested interactive switches.

Pill shapes and circular containers are prohibited, preserving the atelier's architectural language.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid burnished amber background (`#D97706`), raw dark steel typography (`#121214`), 4px border radius, uppercase bold Montserrat (`label-lg`). Hover shifts to brushed bronze (`#E5A93C`) with subtle metallic warmth.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Blackened carbon fill (`#1E1E22`) with 1px concrete outline (`#383840`), light concrete text (`#F2F2F0`). Hover transitions the border to `#D97706`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background with uppercase tracked text and a bottom hairline rule that expands on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact rectangles with 4px radii.
- Inactive: `#1E1E22` background, `#DCDCD8` text, 1px border `#2A2A30`.
- Selected/Active: `#26262B` background with `#D97706` 1px border and amber text accent.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#161619` with a crisp 1px border (`#2E2E35`).
- Text is Work Sans in `#F2F2F0`; placeholder text is muted graphite (`#686874`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#D97706` with an amber ambient field highlight. Labels sit above inputs in uppercase tracked Montserrat (`label-sm`).

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Slate background (`#1E1E22`) encased in a 1px frame (`#2A2A30`).
- Headers feature display typography paired with amber price indicators. Hover states shift the border frame to brushed bronze (`#E5A93C`) without altering element dimensions.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes: 16px square boxes with 2px corner radius; unchecked border `#383840`, checked fill `#D97706` with `#121214` checkmark.
- Radios: 16px squares rotated 45 degrees into diamond geometry or standard micro-rounded boxes containing an inner solid amber square indicator, maintaining the industrial edge.

### Salon-Specific Components
- **Stylist Tier Badge**: High-contrast monolithic tag featuring a hairline bronze perimeter and gold-foil-styled metadata for Master Stylists and Atelier Directors.
- **Time Slot Selector**: Grid matrix of monolithic time blocks: solid carbon slate for available, slashed graphite for booked, and solid amber for selected slots.
